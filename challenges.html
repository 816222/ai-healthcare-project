Problems and Limitations of AI

1. High Cost

AI systems can be expensive to develop, install, and maintain.

2. Privacy and Security

AI uses patient information, so healthcare organizations must protect personal data.

3. Need for Skilled People

Doctors and healthcare workers need proper training to use AI technology.

4. AI Can Make Mistakes

AI may provide incorrect results if the information used is incomplete or incorrect.

5. Limited Access

Some hospitals and rural areas may not have enough technology or internet access.
