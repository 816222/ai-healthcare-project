How AI is Used Around the World

Countries around the world use Artificial Intelligence to improve healthcare services, disease detection, and medical treatment.

1. AI Disease Diagnosis

AI helps doctors detect diseases such as cancer, diabetes, and heart disease earlier.

2. Medical Imaging

AI analyzes X-rays, CT scans, and MRI images to identify health problems.

3. Robotic Surgery

AI-powered robots help surgeons perform complex operations with greater accuracy.

4. Telemedicine

AI supports online healthcare services for patients in remote areas.
